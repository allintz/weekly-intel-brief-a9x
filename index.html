Bright Line Watch Deep Dive

What Is Bright Line Watch?
Bright Line Watch surveys political scientists about the state of US democracy, asking them to evaluate specific democratic practices and norm-adherence. Scores are reported on a 0–100 scale, with 100 representing full adherence to democratic norms. It measures democratic practice rather than institutional structure.
The 0–100 Scale
100 = full adherence to democratic norms and practices. 0 = complete breakdown. The Feb–Mar 2026 wave produced a score of approximately 57/100 (n=621 political scientists), up from the April 2025 low of 53. Experts saw some improvement in judicial checks on the executive; rule-of-law and norm-following dimensions remained significantly depressed.

What BLW Measures Well
  • Specific incidents and norm violations by officials
  • Departure from informal democratic conventions
  • Expert perception of democratic health over time
What BLW Doesn’t Capture Well
  • Structural institutional resilience (courts, federalism, military deference)
  • Public mobilization or civil society response
  • International democratic solidarity mechanisms
  • Path dependency — how hard reversals would be
