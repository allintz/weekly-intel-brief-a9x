Bright Line Watch — Deep Dive

What is Bright Line Watch?
Bright Line Watch is a political science project that surveys expert scholars of democracy on their assessment of how the United States is performing across democratic practices. It measures behavior, not just legal structure — how officials act, whether norms are followed, whether institutions are being respected in practice.
Scale (0–100)
100 = perfect democratic practice; 0 = complete authoritarianism. Scores represent expert-consensus assessment of actual behavior, not legal capacity. A country can score high on V-Dem (institutions still formally intact) while scoring low on BLW (officials ignoring norms those institutions are supposed to enforce).

What the Spring 2026 Wave Found
The Spring 2026 wave documents a continued sharp decline in expert assessments of democratic practice. Key areas driving the score down include: executive compliance with court orders, respect for independent agency authority, adherence to appointment and removal constraints, and protection of civil society organizations from government targeting. The V-Dem parallel classification of the US as "electoral democracy" (rather than liberal democracy) for the first time in 50 years is consistent with the BLW trajectory.
What BLW Measures Well
  • • Expert assessment of current executive behavior relative to democratic norms
  • • Changes in norm compliance over time within the same political system
  • • Early warning signals of democratic backsliding before legal/institutional collapse
What BLW Does Not Capture Well
  • • Public opinion on democratic practices (expert survey only)
  • • Legal/institutional capacity (measured by V-Dem and Freedom House separately)
  • • Economic or material conditions affecting democratic resilience
  • • Sub-national variation (state-level democratic health not captured)
