Bright Line Watch Deep Dive

What is Bright Line Watch?
BLW is a political scientist survey project that tracks democratic erosion in the United States. Expert respondents (political scientists) evaluate US democratic performance on a 0–100 scale across multiple dimensions, conducted in periodic waves. Not a public poll — respondents are academic experts.
Historical Trajectory
WaveScoreNotes

Fall 2020~65Election administration concerns

Summer 2022~60Post-Dobbs, continued norm erosion

Spring 2026~44Steepest single-wave decline; second Trump term actions
What the Spring 2026 Wave Found
~44/100. The Spring 2026 wave reflects the steepest expert-assessed decline in US democratic practice in BLW’s history. Contributors include: executive branch norm violations, judicial targeting, press freedom erosion, and reduction in institutional checks. This is a measure of practice not structure — US laws and constitution remain formally intact.
What BLW Measures Well
  • Executive branch norm compliance
  • Electoral administration integrity
  • Judicial and media independence in practice
What BLW Doesn’t Capture Well
  • Structural/constitutional changes that haven’t yet manifested in practice
  • Sub-national variation (state-level backsliding vs. resistance)
  • Economic or technology-driven erosion vectors
  • Leading indicators (the score reflects current state, not early warning of future erosion)
